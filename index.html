THE PERSON BEHIND THE PRACTICE

A pharmacist’s mind.
A musician’s soul.

Meet Niraj Naik.

Before SOMA Breath, Niraj was a pharmacist and music producer. His own health crisis led him to explore pranayama, meditation and the role of breath in how we feel.

He brought those worlds together to create SOMA Breath: rhythmic breathing, guided meditation and original music, woven into an experience you can follow and feel.

Known as The Renegade Pharmacist, Niraj is also the creator of Mindvalley’s Breathwork for Life and has produced music for Wim Hof Method courses and the app.

ANCIENT ROOTS. A MODERN RHYTHM.

You don’t need
to quiet your mind
before you arrive.

Let the music give you a way in.

SOMA Breath draws on pranayama, the yogic practice of working with the breath. Rhythmic music helps you follow the breathing patterns, while a trained guide leads the session.

It is a practice you take part in, at your own pace. You do not need to be an experienced meditator or dancer to be curious.

Guidance throughout.

Our certified SOMA Breath facilitators will guide you throughout. We’ll share the full line-up and session details with our priority list.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious?
Start here.

What happens at a Breath Rave?+

We’ll start with guided SOMA Breath, breathing in rhythm with the music. Then we’ll take that energy onto the dance floor. Our facilitators and DJs will guide you through, with time to settle and connect afterwards.

Do I need breathwork or dance experience?+

No. We’ll show you how to follow the breathing, and you can move however feels good to you. Come curious, go at your own pace and let the music help you find your feet.

When is it, and how do I get tickets?+

We’re bringing Breath Rave to fabric London in January 2027. Join our priority list and we’ll email you as soon as dates and tickets are released.

Who’s leading the experience?+

Our certified SOMA Breath facilitators and breathwork DJs will guide the session. Join our priority list to be first to hear the full line-up.

Is breathwork suitable for everyone?+

Some breathing techniques and breath holds aren’t suitable for everyone. If you’re pregnant or have a medical or mental health condition, please check with your healthcare professional and speak with us before booking. During the session, you can pause, rest or return to normal breathing whenever you need.

Is this an alcohol-free event?+

Yes. We’re keeping the dance floor alcohol-free, with the focus on breathwork, music and connection. We’ll have soft drinks and refreshments available.
