A DIFFERENT WAY TO GO OUT

Out of your head.
Into the moment.

You know that feeling when the music takes over and everything else falls away?

Now bring your breath into it.

Breath Rave brings together guided rhythmic breathing and a dance floor built for letting go. Start on the mat. Follow the music. See where the experience takes you.

01
THE BREATH

Drop into yourself.

Follow the guidance and breathe in rhythm with the music. There is no need to work it all out in your head. You are guided through the practice.

02
THE MUSIC

Let your body lead.

The energy rises and the room opens into dance. Electronic music, freedom to move and a shared rhythm. No choreography. No performance.

03
THE CONNECTION

Take it all in.

Make space to settle, notice how you feel and connect with the people around you. Your experience is your own. There is no right way to feel.

ANCIENT ROOTS. A MODERN RHYTHM.

You don’t need
to quiet your mind
before you arrive.

Let the music give you a way in.

SOMA Breath draws on pranayama, the yogic practice of working with the breath. Rhythmic music helps you follow the breathing patterns, while a trained guide leads the session.

It is a practice you take part in, at your own pace. You do not need to be an experienced meditator or dancer to be curious.

Guidance throughout.

Our certified SOMA Breath facilitators will guide you throughout. We’ll share the full line-up and session details with our priority list.

Is this a night out?+

No. Breath Rave runs in the daytime. You’ll be dancing in daylight and home in good time, with none of the 3am and none of the comedown.

Where is it?+

An iconic London nightclub, the kind of room you’ve probably danced in before. We name it to the priority list first, along with the date. One day, one room, limited capacity, so the list gets the first shot at tickets.

What happens at a Breath Rave?+

We start on the floor with guided SOMA Breath, breathing in rhythm with the music. Then the energy lifts and the room moves into dance. Facilitators and breathwork DJs guide the whole way, with time to settle and connect afterwards.

Do I need breathwork or dance experience?+

No. We’ll show you how to follow the breathing, and you can move however feels good to you. Come curious, go at your own pace and let the music help you find your feet.

When is it, and how do I get tickets?+

January 2027, in London. We’re confirming the date now. The priority list books first when tickets go live, and we’ll email you before the drop.

Who’s leading the experience?+

Our certified SOMA Breath facilitators and breathwork DJs will guide the session. Join our priority list to be first to hear the full line-up.

Is breathwork suitable for everyone?+

Some breathing techniques and breath holds aren’t suitable for everyone. If you’re pregnant or have a medical or mental health condition, please check with your healthcare professional and speak with us before booking. During the session, you can pause, rest or return to normal breathing whenever you need.

Is the venue accessible?+

Tell us what you need at info@houseofjung.org and we’ll confirm exactly what the venue can offer before you book. Full access details go out with the venue announcement.

Is this an alcohol-free event?+

Yes. We’re keeping the dance floor alcohol-free, with the focus on breathwork, music and connection. We’ll have soft drinks and refreshments available.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE PRACTICE

Meet Niraj Naik.

A UK pharmacist whose own health crisis led him to breathwork. He built SOMA Breath out of pranayama, guided meditation and his own music.

Known as The Renegade Pharmacist, he created Mindvalley’s Breathwork for Life and produced music for Wim Hof Method courses.
